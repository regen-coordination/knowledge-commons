# Regen Knowledge Garden

The Regen Knowledge Garden is a developing knowledge environment for regenerative communities and public-goods work. Its purpose is to help people find useful ideas, tools, methods, source systems, projects, organizations, people, and lessons; understand how they relate; and build on them with their sources and context intact.

A Garden is more than a searchable list. Knowledge needs tending: attribution, corrections, care for local and community context, distinctions between claims and evidence, and clear limits on what has been reviewed or can be reused.

- [Regen Knowledge Garden on Geo](https://www.geobrowser.io/space/b818ec4b89d949d88342249e3c649cd0) - a current graph and browsing space, including a [Start here](https://www.geobrowser.io/space/b818ec4b89d949d88342249e3c649cd0?tabId=0b5ebbd374f0425781308c2cba87fdf2) orientation.

- [Knowledge Commons on Geo](https://www.geobrowser.io/space/bd727a6ad6ec4a058f681ea9002a1fbf) - a distinct shared space for knowledge about commoning and reusable semantic structures.

- [Regen Knowledge Commons repository](../README.md) - supporting documentation, Geo tools, and developing ingestion and review approaches.

Scope matters: the shared Commons is meant to provide reusable commoning knowledge and structures. The Regen Garden is a Regen-specific environment that can build on them. Geo is an important current surface, not the entire Garden or a commitment to a single long-term storage system.

## What may grow here

The Garden can connect knowledge across source systems and communities, including:

- Organizations, people, projects, tools, networks, and places working on regenerative and public-goods aims.

- Concepts, thinkers, movement and community lineages, methods, protocols, and practical guides.

- Source systems such as gardens, repositories, governance forums, research archives, maps, datasets, and media collections, with their stewardship and reuse conditions visible.

- Case studies, implementation learning, failures, cautions, review questions, and evidence associated with particular claims.

Possible inputs include the independent [Regen Toolkit](https://github.com/regen-coordination/regen-toolkit), the July 2026 research handoff and raw migration corpus, selected learning from [Regen Coordination OS](https://github.com/regen-coordination/regen-coordination-os), and peer commons. These are sources and relationships to cultivate, not a statement that the material has already been imported, reviewed, or synchronized.

See the [project map and source lineage](MAP.md) for where those artifacts live and how they relate.

## How this work is stewarded

A source system is not just a URL. Where possible, Garden records should preserve the source, its steward and context, attribution, relevant rights or public-use limitations, and a path for corrections. Sensitive community, movement, and place-based knowledge requires particular care; public availability alone does not authorize unrestricted reuse or representation.

Being discoverable is not the same as being accepted or approved. Some Garden content is publicly indexed, while a [release-review record](https://www.geobrowser.io/space/b818ec4b89d949d88342249e3c649cd0/643764af75fb4967addab012cd99aeb6) still describes local draft and unpublished status. That wording needs maintainer clarification. Until then, do not infer editorial approval from indexing, and do not treat raw or AI-assisted material as human-reviewed knowledge.

To flag a documentation issue or suggest a source or correction, use the [Knowledge Commons repository issues](https://github.com/regen-coordination/knowledge-commons/issues) or contact the relevant maintainers. This is an orientation route, not an automatic ingestion or publication workflow. Edits to the shared Geo space follow the separately documented [Geo operating and governance procedures](geo/operations.md).

## What remains open

Contribution and review workflows, semantic extensions, connections to source systems, claims and evidence practices, public interfaces, agent workflows, and federation remain areas for investigation and human decisions. This page gives the Garden a discoverable place in the project map; it does not define a final ontology, approved architecture, or launch status.
